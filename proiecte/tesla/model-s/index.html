Exterior

Drawn by the wind.

A long, low silhouette with one of the lowest drag figures of any production car. Fewer seams, fewer lines, nothing that doesn’t need to be there.

Lighting

A face you know at night.

Slim LED headlights up front and a full-width light bar at the back, so the car is unmistakable in the dark.

Interior

A 17-inch screen, room to stretch.

A yoke or a round wheel, ventilated seats, a second screen for the rear and a cabin that stays quiet at any speed.
