Exterior

One line, nose to tail.

The refreshed body is sharper and lower, with flush door handles, an all-glass roof and slim lights at both ends.

Lighting

Slimmer lights, sharper look.

New matrix LED headlights and a C-shaped light bar at the rear. The tail lights are projected through the glass.

Interior

A cabin built around one screen.

Ambient lighting that wraps the cabin, ventilated front seats, and an 8-inch screen for the rear seats.
