Exterior

Stainless steel, no paint.

An exoskeleton made of thick stainless steel sheet. Hard to dent, it never needs a paint job, and it looks like nothing else on the road.

Lighting

One line of light.

A single LED strip runs across the full width of the front, and another across the back.

Interior

A huge screen and a lot of glass.

An 18.5-inch touchscreen, a yoke-style wheel and a glass roof that runs the length of the cabin.
