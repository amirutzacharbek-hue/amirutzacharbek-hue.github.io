Exterior

Higher seat, same sports-car feel.

A low centre of gravity keeps it flat through corners, while the higher seating position gives you a better view of the road.

Lighting

A light bar you notice.

Full-width lighting front and rear makes the car easy to recognise from far away.

Interior

Seats that fold flat.

Fold the rear seats and the floor is flat from the tailgate to the front seats. Big enough for a bike, a sofa or a camping trip.
