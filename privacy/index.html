Privacy

In short: this website does not collect any data about you.

What this site collects

There are no forms, no cookies and no analytics or tracking scripts (no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, nothing similar). Fonts, scripts and 3D files are all served from this same website, without third-party services.

One exception: on the Starbucks concept page you can open a Google Maps view to find stores near you. It only loads after you click, and only then does Google receive your request (and your location, if you choose to share it), under Google's privacy policy.

If you email me

If you send me an email, I receive your address and your message. I use them only to reply and, if we work together, to deliver your project. I don't share them and I don't send newsletters or ads.

I keep emails for as long as we are talking or working together. You can ask me to delete them at any time at amirutzacharbek@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its use. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may briefly keep visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for technical and security reasons.

Concept projects

The Tesla and Starbucks pages are unofficial concept work made for this portfolio. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc. or Starbucks Corporation. Nothing you type on them is sent anywhere.
