Exterior

Doors that open upwards.

Falcon Wing doors rise straight up, so the second and third rows are easy to reach even in a tight parking space.

Lighting

Big glass, bright cabin.

A panoramic windshield runs back over the front seats, so the view up is as good as the view ahead.

Interior

Room for seven.

Three rows of seats, a yoke or a wheel, and a screen for the passengers in the back.
