01 / Automotive

Five 3D showrooms you scroll through

Pick a Model S, 3, X, Y or the Cybertruck and get its own page. Detailed real-time 3D models sit on a mirror floor, and as you scroll the camera circles the car, lights it up at night and flies inside the cabin so you can look around. Real photos fill the gallery, and at the end you change the paint and lighting live.

Your name on a 3D cup

What I care about is the feeling a site leaves behind: the first impression, the small motion that makes a button satisfying, a page that opens instantly on a slow connection. I handle the whole thing, from design to code to launch.
